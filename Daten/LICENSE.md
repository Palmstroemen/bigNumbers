You may use, share and adapt it for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit, for example: “Daten: bigNumbers (https://github.com/Palmstroemen/bigNumbers), CC BY 4.0”, and keep the sources named in each entry.

Exceptions:

* `bilder/` – each picture keeps the license of its author as stated in the entry's `image` field (CC BY, CC BY-SA or public domain, from Wikimedia Commons).
* `source_quotes.json` – the literal quotes belong to their sources; they are short excerpts kept to document where a number comes from.
* Figures taken from Eurostat, the ECB, the World Bank, national statistics offices and other sources remain subject to their terms; each entry names its source.
